## शिवकुमारः
Source: [TW](https://x.com/SaamaanyaJ/status/2069671214382194999)

Vyasa says
1. Karna's father Adhiratha was Dhritarashtra's friend.
2. Karna joined Duryodhana much earlier, 'imbibed' all his hatred for Pandavas and was part of the gang that pushed Bheema into Ganga waters.
3. Karna was taught by Drona alongside Kauravas and Pandavas.
4. Karna was denied Brahmastra by Drona only because of his hatred for Arjuna and larger character.
